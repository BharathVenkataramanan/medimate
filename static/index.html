Settings & privacy

Reminders

Allow browser notifications while this website is open, or export reminders to your device calendar.

Optional local helper. A private copy of medicine names, dose quantities, instructions, and schedules is kept on this computer for reminders. Notifications may reveal these details on the lock screen.

Your data

Your medicine entries and dose confirmations are stored locally in this browser. There is no account or cloud synchronization.

Medicine or ingredient names may be checked online. Family names, doses and dose histories are not shared for these checks.

Interaction data: DDInter 2.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial use).

AI & privacy

AI questions are sent to OpenAI for processing. The medication registry is never included automatically. The API request sets store:false, but standard processing and retention policies may still apply.

Stop MediMate

Closing the browser or restarting the computer keeps enabled background reminders active. Use this button to stop MediMate and reminders without deleting medicines.
